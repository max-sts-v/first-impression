Verb + Preposition Collocations

Study these common collocations. You will use them in the task.
come up to
To approach someone or something.
“She came up to me and asked for directions.”
go out with
To date someone; to have a romantic relationship.
“He is going out with a colleague from work.”
listen to
To give attention to sound or someone speaking.
“Please listen to me carefully.”
look at
To direct your eyes towards something.
“Look at that beautiful painting.”
spend time on
To use time to do something.
“I don't want to spend too much time on this.”
talk to
To speak with someone.
“You should talk to your manager about it.”
wait for
To stay until someone or something arrives.
“I will wait for you at the station.”
